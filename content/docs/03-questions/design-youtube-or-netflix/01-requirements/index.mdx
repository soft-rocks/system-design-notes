## Functional Requirements

- **Video Upload and Playback** — Users can upload videos (producers) or browse and watch videos (viewers)
- **Adaptive Bitrate Streaming** — Videos are transcoded into multiple resolutions and formats (360p, 480p, 720p, 1080p, 4K) using HLS or DASH, allowing dynamic quality switching based on network conditions
- **Search and Discovery** — Full-text search across video titles, tags, and descriptions; personalized recommendations based on watch history, preferences, and similar user behavior
- **User Engagement** — User accounts with sign up/login, subscriptions, playlists, watch history, likes/dislikes, and comments
- **Live Streaming** — Real-time streaming support with low-latency delivery and live viewer counts
- **Content Moderation** — Automated detection (hash matching, ML-based content analysis) combined with manual review for flagged content
- **Monetization** — Ad insertion (pre-roll, mid-roll, display banners), subscription tiers (Netflix-style), or revenue sharing with creators (YouTube-style)

## Non-Functional Requirements

- **Availability** — 99.9%+ uptime; video playback must not fail during a session
- **Latency** — Video playback begins within 2 seconds; upload acknowledgment within seconds; search results in under 200ms
- **Scalability** — Support 10 million concurrent streams globally with ability to handle 3x peak traffic during evening hours
- **Storage** — Efficiently manage 100M+ videos with multiple transcoded versions and replicas across regions
- **Security** — DRM for premium content, JWT-based authentication, DDoS protection, rate limiting on APIs
- **Global Reach** — Sub-100ms latency to end users via CDN edge locations in every major region

## Scale Assumptions

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Monthly active users | 2 billion |
| Video upload rate | 500 hours/minute |
| Daily watch time | 1 billion hours |
| Concurrent streams | 10 million |
| Average video size | 500 MB (1080p, 1 hour) |
| Average session length | 40 minutes |
| Raw storage | ~50 PB |
| Total storage (with replicas + transcodes) | ~200 PB |
| Read-to-write ratio | 100,000:1 |
| Peak traffic multiplier | 3x average |
