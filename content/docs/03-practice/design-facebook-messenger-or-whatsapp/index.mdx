## Overview

Facebook Messenger and WhatsApp are messaging platforms that enable real-time text, voice, and video communication at global scale. Both services must deliver messages in under a second to billions of users across varying network conditions, while supporting features like group chats, media sharing, end-to-end encryption, and multi-device sync. This design focuses on the shared architectural patterns underlying such platforms.
