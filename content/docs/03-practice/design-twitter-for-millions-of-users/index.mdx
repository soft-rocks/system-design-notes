This design covers a Twitter-like microblogging platform serving millions of daily active users with billions of reads and hundreds of millions of tweets per day.

- [High-Level Design](05-high-level-design/) — Architecture overview, component diagram, technology choices
- [Deep Dives](06-deep-dives/) — Feed fan-out, tweet deduplication, real-time notifications, trending topics
