## Two-Stage Retrieval

**Stage 1 — Retrieval (Candidate Generation):** Narrows ~M candidates to ~500 using multi-armed bandit exploration + collaborative filtering.

Sources of candidates:
- Hashtags the user has engaged with recently
- Similar accounts (overlapping engagement patterns)
- Trending content in the user's geographic region
- Suggested accounts the user doesn't follow

**Stage 2 — Ranking:** Gradient-boosted decision tree (XGBoost/LightGBM) predicts P(engage) for each candidate. Returns top ~50.

**Why two stages?** Full ranking over millions of candidates is too slow for the 200ms SLA. Retrieval narrows to a manageable set; ranking fine-sorts.

## Feature Engineering

| Category | Features |
| --- | --- |
| **Photo** | Engagement velocity (likes/min), image quality score, content category (ML classification), creator engagement rate, time since upload |
| **User** | Historical engagement with similar content, past interaction with creator, session length (early = broad, late = narrow) |
| **Context** | Time of day, day of week, network type (WiFi/cellular), engagement mode (explore vs. check-in) |

## Ranking Model

The model predicts a composite engagement score:

```
score = w1 × P(like) + w2 × P(comment) + w3 × P(save) + w4 × P(time_spent > 3s)
```

Weights are tuned to maximize **long-term user engagement (DAU retention)**, not just short-term clicks. The `P(save)` signal is particularly important — saves indicate high-quality content.

**Training:** Retrained daily using a labeled dataset of interactions over the past 30 days. Positive = like/comment/save/>3s view. Negative = scroll past.

**Serving:** TensorFlow Serving microservice. Inference &lt;5ms per candidate. 500 candidates scored in parallel.

## Diversity & Freshness

**Diversity constraints (post-processing):**
- At most 30% from a single content category
- At most 2 photos from the same creator
- Limited photos with the same hashtag

**Exploration vs. exploitation:** 10% of Explore slots reserved for exploration (new categories, new creators). Thompson Sampling dynamically adjusts exploration rate per user.

**Freshness boost:** Photos uploaded within 24 hours receive a 1.2× multiplicative score boost.

**Negative feedback:**
- Swipe past: category/creator decay 0.5× for 24 hours
- "Not Interested": 0.2× for 7 days
