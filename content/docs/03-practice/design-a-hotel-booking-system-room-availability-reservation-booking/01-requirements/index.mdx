## Functional Requirements

### Room Catalog Management
- Multi-property hotel catalogs with hierarchical structure: Hotel → Property → Floor → Room Type → Room Instance.
- Room types define attributes: bed type, occupancy limits, size, amenities, view, accessibility features.
- Room instances are individual bookable units (e.g., Room 507) with physical state (clean, dirty, under maintenance).
- Multiple rate plans per room type: standard, member discount, corporate, seasonal, flash sale.

### Availability Management
- Real-time availability lookup by date range, room type, occupancy (guests/children).
- Availability = booked inventory − blocked rooms (maintenance, out-of-order) + overbooking buffer.
- Support room-type-level availability (upgrades/downgrades within type group) and room-instance-level exact matches.
- Hold windows: lock availability for X minutes during checkout flow.

### Reservation Booking
- Create reservations with guest info, check-in/out dates, room type, rate plan, special requests.
- Multi-room bookings (same or different types) and multi-property stay packages.
- Booking lifecycle: Pending → Confirmed → Checked-In → Checked-Out → Completed; with cancellable and no-show states.
- Partial cancellations: cancel specific rooms or the entire booking.

### Pricing & Rate Calculation
- Total price = nightly rates × nights + taxes + fees (resort, occupancy, service) + addons (breakfast, parking).
- Dynamic pricing: demand-based adjustments, advance purchase discounts, last-minute deals, length-of-stay discounts.
- Rate hold: lock rate for 15 minutes during checkout to prevent race conditions.

### Payment Processing
- Pre-authorization at booking time (hold amount on guest card).
- Capture at check-in or checkout depending on policy.
- Split payments: multiple payment methods per reservation.
- Partial/full refunds with policy enforcement.

### Inventory Protection & Overbooking Control
- Configurable overbooking buffer per room type based on no-show historical rates.
- Walk policy: when overbooked, relocate guest to another property with compensation.
- Blocked room management: maintenance, renovations, VIP holds, group blocks.

### Group & Block Booking
- Block bookings for events/conferences/weddings (20+ rooms).
- Block release deadlines: rooms auto-release back to inventory if unconfirmed.
- Group rate negotiation and custom contracts.

### Cancellation & Modification
- Flexible/moderate/strikethrough cancellation policies per rate plan.
- Modification window: allow date/room changes without penalty within policy.
- No-show handling: charge no-show fee and release inventory.

### Guest & Loyalty Integration
- Guest profiles with preferences, history, and loyalty tier.
- Loyalty points accrual and redemption per stay.
- Corporate account billing and direct billing for B2B partners.

### Search & Discovery
- Search by location (geospatial), dates, room type, price range, amenities, ratings.
- Filters: free cancellation, breakfast included, pet-friendly, refundable deposit.
- Ranking by relevance, price, rating, and commission rate.

### Notification & Communication
- Booking confirmation, modification, and cancellation via email/SMS/push.
- Pre-arrival reminders (24h) and checkout receipts.
- Overbooking/walk notifications to affected guests.

### Reporting & Analytics
- Occupancy rate, RevPAR (Revenue Per Available Room), ADR (Average Daily Rate).
- Demand forecasting by date, room type, property.
- Channel performance: direct vs. OTA (Online Travel Agency) breakdown.

### Channel Manager Integration
- Bidirectional inventory/rate sync across channels: own site, Expedia, Booking.com, Airbnb, GDS.
- New bookings from any channel immediately reduce available inventory.
- Rate parity enforcement across channels.

### Check-In / Check-Out Management
- Front-desk operations: ID verification, deposit collection, room assignment.
- Early check-in / late check-out with availability-based approval and extra charges.
- Digital check-in via mobile app with keyless entry integration.

## Non-Functional Requirements

### Availability & Latency
- 99.99% availability during peak booking seasons.
- Search query p95 latency < 200ms (including geospatial filtering).
- Booking confirmation p95 latency < 500ms (end-to-end including payment auth).

### Consistency
- **Strong consistency for inventory**: double-booking must never occur under any condition.
- Strong consistency for payment transactions (ACID).
- Eventual consistency for reporting, analytics, and notifications.

### Throughput
- 50K booking search queries/sec during peak (500K during flash sales).
- 5K booking creation transactions/sec peak (50K during flash sales).
- 200K inventory sync messages/sec from channel managers and PMS.

### Data Integrity & Durability
- Zero double-bookings: the hardest constraint, enforced via single-partition writes per room type per date.
- PCI-DSS compliance for payment data (encrypted at rest and in transit).
- Audit log: all booking state transitions, rate changes, and cancellations retained for 7 years.

### Scalability
- Horizontal scaling for search and booking services independently.
- 100K+ active room types globally with sub-millisecond availability checks.
- Multi-region: active-active for search (read-heavy), active-passive for bookings (write-heavy, consistency-critical).

### Extensibility
- Plugin-based rate engine for new pricing strategies without code deployment.
- PMS integration SDK in multiple languages.
- Payment method abstraction for new methods (Apple Pay, WeChat Pay, crypto).

### Operational
- Real-time monitoring: booking success rate, inventory sync lag, payment failure rate.
- Circuit breakers for downstream dependencies (payment gateway, channel managers).
- 1-minute rollback window via idempotent reversal API.

## Scale Assumptions

### Market Scale
- 50,000 partner hotels × 100 avg rooms = **5M total rooms**.
- 100M monthly active users.
- 50K peak search queries/sec; 5K booking transactions/sec peak.

### Inventory & Storage
- **Reservation records**: 5M rooms × 365 days × 2 years = 3.65B records → ~7.3TB (2KB/record).
- **Search index**: 5M room types × 50K date windows = 250B entries → ~25TB.
- **Daily growth**: ~50GB reservation records + ~10GB log events.

### Traffic Mix
- Search : Booking : Modification : Cancellation ≈ 50:1:0.2:0.3.
- Mobile 70%, Web 20%, Partner APIs 10%.
- Direct 60%, OTA 40%.

### Geographic Distribution
- NA 40%, Europe 30%, APAC 25%, Rest 5%.
- Cross-region bookings: ~10%.
- Data residency: GDPR (EU), CCPA (California).
